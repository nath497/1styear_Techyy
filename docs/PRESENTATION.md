# TutorMatch — Session 3 presentation script

*Find Your Match. Learn Together. Grow Together.*

About **6 minutes** in total: pitch (~2 min), live demo (~3 min), close
(~1 min), then questions. If your time limit is 3 minutes, use the short
script in `docs/PITCH.md` instead.

Replace **Speaker 1 / 2 / 3** with your names. Lines in *italics* are
what you **do**; plain text is what you **say**. Anything in
**[brackets]** is a blank for real results — fill it in, or say the
honest version.

## Part 1 — The pitch (~2 minutes)

### The person — Speaker 1

This is Josh Lavado. Josh is a college student, and this year
Mathematics in the Modern World has been hard for him. Some lessons
just don't click. He doesn't need a new course. He needs someone to
explain it clearly.

So Josh searched online, then asked a senior he knows. In his words,
it "kinda worked." It took him three hours to understand one lesson.
And his senior? Always busy.

Josh's help depends on someone else's free time.

### Today — Speaker 1

Josh isn't alone. Jamaica studies with her notes. It works a little.
Angel reviews with a friend, and is still stuck. Neither pays anything
today.

But all three told us they would pay a classmate to tutor them. Josh,
up to one hundred pesos. Jamaica and Angel, up to one hundred fifty.

### Our fix — Speaker 2

TutorMatch helps learners find fellow students who can tutor them,
within their budget.

Josh doesn't need to already know someone. He tells TutorMatch his
subject and his budget, and it shows him tutors who fit. He books a
session, chats with his tutor, joins a video call, and plans his
studying — all in one place.

Let us show you.

## Part 2 — The live demo (~3 minutes) — Speaker 2 and Speaker 3

### 1. BudgetMatch (30s) — Speaker 2

*On Josh's account: open **Find a Tutor**. Choose Mathematics, College,
type 100.*

This is BudgetMatch. Josh picks his subject, his level, and his budget:
one hundred pesos.

*Point at a result's match line.*

He only sees tutors who fit, and each one tells him why. One hundred
pesos, within your budget. Teaches Mathematics. Face-to-face.

And notice who's missing: we have a tutor who charges one hundred fifty
pesos. They don't show up for Josh, because that's over his budget.

### 2. Request a session (20s) — Speaker 2

*Open Tutor A's page. Fill in topic "Quadratic Equations", a date and a
time. Press **Request a session**. Open **My Sessions**.*

Josh sends a session request: his topic, the date and the time. It's
now Pending. And a tutor can never be double-booked for the same hour —
the database checks that itself.

### 3. The tutor accepts (20s) — Speaker 3

*Switch to Tutor A. Open **My Sessions**. Press **Accept**. Paste the
Google Meet link and save.*

Now I'm the tutor. I see Josh's request and accept it. Since we're
meeting online, I add a Google Meet link.

### 4. Video call and chat (30s) — Speaker 2

*Back on Josh: show **Join video call** on the session. Open
**Messages**, send "Hi! Can we start with factoring?"*

Josh now has a Join video call button. And he can message his tutor
right here —

*Show the message appearing on Tutor A's screen.*

— it arrives live, no refresh. Only the two of them can read this chat.

### 5. My Learning Space (30s) — Speaker 2

*On Josh: open **My Learning Space**, then the **LearnTrack** tab.*

TutorMatch also helps Josh between sessions. This is My Learning Space.
In LearnTrack, Josh marked Quadratic Equations as "Need to Review."

*Tap **Create study task** on that topic, set tomorrow's date, press
**Add task**.*

One tap turns it into a study task for tomorrow.

*Open **Session Notes**. On the session, tap **Add from LearnTrack**,
type a question, press **Save notes**.*

And before the session, he writes his questions. "Add from LearnTrack"
fills in the topics he's stuck on. These notes are private — even the
tutor can't see them.

### 6. Completed and the 10% (20s) — Speaker 3

*Switch to Tutor A. Press **Mark completed**. Show the commission line.*

After the session, the tutor marks it completed. TutorMatch records its
ten percent: ten pesos for us, ninety for the tutor. Recorded, not
collected. We don't handle real money yet.

### 7. Our business model (15s) — Speaker 3

*On Josh: open **Subscription**. Tap **Subscribe** on TutorMatch Plus.*

How we'll grow: the core stays free. Later, learners can choose
TutorMatch Plus for forty-nine pesos a month, and tutors Tutor Pro for
ninety-nine. This is a prototype — as you can see, nothing is charged.

## Part 3 — Safety and close (~1 minute) — Speaker 1

### Safety

Everything you saw is private by default, and the database itself
enforces it — not just the screens. A second learner can't see Josh's
sessions, chats or study notes, and even his tutor can't read his
private notes. We tested this by trying to break in, and every attempt
was refused.

### How we'll know

We asked Josh, Jamaica and Angel to try it on the live site.
**[Say what happened: who found a tutor within budget, who sent a real
request, and what got in the way.]** **[Number]** classmates signed up
as real tutors.

If learners find a tutor and send real requests without needing to
know anyone, it worked. If they don't, we'll find out why.

### Closing line

Josh shouldn't have to wait for a busy senior to understand his lesson.
With TutorMatch, help is a search away — within his budget.

Thank you. We're happy to take questions.

## Part 4 — Questions you'll probably get

### Q1 — "They *said* they'd pay. Did anyone actually book?"

"No money has changed hands — we deliberately record the commission
instead of collecting it. What we can show is **[who sent a real
request on the live site]**. That's our strongest evidence so far, and
the next step is a real paid session."

### Q2 — "You built a lot. Isn't this too much for a first version?"

"The core — BudgetMatch, a request, accept, complete, the 10% — was
live first. Everything else came after it worked, one small piece at a
time, each checked on a phone. If we had to cut, we'd keep the core and
the chat, because that's what gets Josh a tutor."

### Q3 — "Your learners don't pay a tutor today. Why would they pay ₱49 a month?"

"Fair — we don't know yet, and that's why it's a prototype and nothing
is charged. Our first income is the 10% on completed sessions, which
only happens when learners already got help. Plus is something we'll
test after the core has real users."

### Q4 — "Where do the tutors come from? Why tutor for ₱100 minus your 10%?"

"For now we're recruiting classmates who are good at a subject. Tutors
set their own rate, and ninety pesos for an hour is real pocket money
for a student. The commission is only recorded, so nobody loses money
while we learn whether tutors will join."

### Q5 — "How safe are users' accounts?"

"Passwords are handled by Supabase and stored scrambled — we never see
them. Every table has Row Level Security, so the database decides who
can see each row: your sessions, chats, study plan and notes are yours
alone. A user's role can never be changed, and our secret database keys
never reach the browser."

### Q6 — "What would you add before a real launch?"

"Three things we left out on purpose to keep the demo simple: turning
email confirmation back on, a forgot-password flow, and later two-step
login. Then real payments through a provider like GCash."

## If something breaks during the demo

Stay calm and say what *should* happen: "Normally, the tutor would now
see the request." Then switch to your screen recording and carry on.
Facilitators care more about how you handle it than about a perfect
click-through.
