# 26 — Session Notes: prepare for and reflect on real sessions

**What to build:** the Session Notes tab lists the learner's real
Accepted and Completed bookings (date and time, tutor, subject). For
each, they write questions to ask and topics they need help with — with
an "add from LearnTrack" button that inserts their Need to Review topic
names for that subject — and, once the session time has passed, a short
reflection. Notes are private to the learner.
