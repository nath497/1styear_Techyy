# 21 — My Learning Space: one page, tabs and the Overview

**What to build:** a learner opens one sidebar link, "My Learning
Space ♡", instead of Study Planner and LearnTrack. The page has six
tabs — Overview, Study Tasks, Schedule, LearnTrack, Goals, Session
Notes — that scroll sideways on a phone. Study Tasks and LearnTrack work
exactly as the Study Planner and LearnTrack do today. Old Study Planner
and LearnTrack links open the matching tab. The Overview shows real
figures: tasks due in the next 7 days, completed tasks, topics at Need
to Review, upcoming tutoring sessions, and overall progress (% of topics
Understood), plus today's tasks. Schedule, Goals and Session Notes say
"coming next" until their tickets are built.
