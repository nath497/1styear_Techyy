# 25 — Goals: progress from linked tasks

**What to build:** in the Goals tab a learner creates goals — title,
optional description, target date — that are Active or Achieved. Study
tasks can be linked to a goal, and the goal shows "X of Y linked tasks
completed" as a progress bar ("Link study tasks to track progress" when
none are linked). The learner taps "Mark achieved" when done. Active
goals appear on the Overview.
