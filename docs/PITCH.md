# TutorMatch — Session 3 pitch

Three minutes, spoken. Replace **Speaker 1 / 2 / 3** with your names.
If one person presents, they read it all. Anything in **[brackets]** is
a blank for real results — fill it in, or say the honest version.

## The script

### 1. The person (~45s) — Speaker 1

This is Josh Lavado. Josh is a college student, and this year
Mathematics in the Modern World has been hard for him. Some lessons
just don't click. He doesn't need a new course. He needs someone to
explain it clearly.

So Josh searched online, then asked a senior he knows. In his words,
it "kinda worked." It took him three hours to understand one lesson.
And his senior? Always busy.

Josh's help depends on someone else's free time.

### 2. Today (~30s) — Speaker 1

Josh isn't alone. Jamaica studies with her notes. It works a little.
Angel reviews with a friend, and is still stuck. Neither pays anything
today.

But all three told us they would pay a classmate to tutor them. Josh,
up to one hundred pesos. Jamaica and Angel, up to one hundred fifty.

### 3. Our fix (~45s) — Speaker 2

TutorMatch helps learners find fellow students who can tutor them,
within their budget.

Josh picks Mathematics, College, one hundred pesos. BudgetMatch shows
only tutors who fit, and tells him why. He sends a session request. The
tutor accepts and adds a Google Meet link. They can chat right in the
app.

And Josh doesn't stop there. In My Learning Space he marks Quadratic
Equations as "Need to Review," turns it into a study task, and writes
his questions before the session.

### 4. The demo moment (~30s) — Speaker 3

Here it is, live, on our real website. Josh's search: a tutor who
charges one hundred fifty pesos doesn't show up. Josh sends a request.
The tutor accepts, then marks it completed. TutorMatch records ten
percent: ten pesos for us, ninety for the tutor. Recorded, not
collected. We don't handle real money yet.

Our plan to grow: the core stays free, with optional plans. This is a
prototype. Nothing is charged.

### 5. How we'll know (~30s) — Speaker 3

We asked Josh, Jamaica and Angel to try it on the live site. **[Say
what happened: who found a tutor within budget, who sent a real
request, and what got in the way.]**

**[Number]** classmates signed up as real tutors. Every table is
private by default: a second learner can't see Josh's sessions, notes
or study plan.

If learners find a tutor and send real requests without needing to
know anyone, it worked. If they don't, we'll tell you why.

*Script length: ~360 words plus your real results in beat 5 — about
three minutes at a calm pace. Keep beat 5 under 80 words.*

## Where your evidence stands

Since Session 1 you built far more than the MVP: the core, plus help
requests, chat, video links, dashboards, My Learning Space and a
subscription prototype. All of it is live and checked on a phone.

The evidence for the **problem** is still what three learners **said**.
What will make this pitch strong is one real thing they **did**: Josh,
Jamaica or Angel finding a tutor and sending a real request on the live
site. Do that before you present, and put the result in beat 5. If it
didn't happen, say so plainly — facilitators trust "we haven't tested
that yet" far more than a vague "users loved it." Also check that
Jamaica and Angel are happy to be named.

## The three hardest questions

### Q1 — "They *said* they'd pay. Did anyone actually book and pay?"

This attacks the weakest point in `docs/PROBLEM.md`: every number is
something people said, not something they did.

*Honest answer:* "No money has changed hands — we deliberately record
the commission instead of collecting it. What we can show is
**[who sent a real request on the live site]**. That's the strongest
evidence we have so far, and the next step is a real paid session."

### Q2 — "You built a lot. Isn't this too much for a first version?"

*Honest answer:* "The core — BudgetMatch, a request, accept, complete,
the 10% — was live first. Everything else came after it worked, one
small piece at a time, each checked on a phone. If we had to cut, we'd
keep the core and the chat, because that's what gets Josh a tutor."

### Q3 — "Your learners won't even pay a tutor today. Why would anyone pay ₱49 a month for Plus?"

*Honest answer:* "Fair — we don't know yet, and that's why it's a
prototype and nothing is charged. Our first income is the 10% on
completed sessions, which only happens when learners already got help.
Plus is an idea we'll test only after the core has real users."
